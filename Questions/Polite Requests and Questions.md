| Expression                     | Meaning in Spanish      | Formality               | Example                                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Would you like to...?**      | ¿Te gustaría...?        | Polite / Professional   | _Would you like to join the call?_     |
| **Would you like to know...?** | ¿Te gustaría saber...?  | Professional & Informal | _Would you like to know what I found?_ |
| **Would you like me to...?**   | ¿Te gustaría que yo...? | Very common at work     | _Would you like me to explain it?_     |
| **Do you want to...?**         | ¿Quieres...?            | Informal                | _Do you want to continue?_             |
| **Could you...?**              | ¿Podrías...?            | Professional & Informal | _Could you share the logs?_            |
| **Can you...?**                | ¿Puedes...?             | Informal                | _Can you help me with this?_           |
| **Would you mind...?**         | ¿Te importaría...?      | Professional            | _Would you mind sharing the file?_     |
| **Shall we...?**               | ¿Continuamos? / ¿Vamos? | Professional & Informal | _Shall we get back to the question?_   |
| **Let's... shall we?**         | Hagámoslo, ¿te parece?  | Natural                 | _Let's continue, shall we?_            |
| **I'd like to...**             | Me gustaría...          | Professional & Informal | _I'd like to improve my English._      |
| **I'd like you to...**         | Me gustaría que tú...   | Professional            | _I'd like you to review the report._   |
| **Can I...?**                  | ¿Puedo...?              | Common                  | _Can I ask a question?_                |
| **May I...?**                  | ¿Me permite...?         | Formal                  | _May I add something?_                 |
